OpenLadder

Free PLC ladder logic simulator. Nothing is locked.

Inputs (tap to switch)

Outputs

Contacts: NO, NC, P (rising edge), N (falling edge). Use the parallel button on a contact to add an OR branch. Outputs: OUT, SET, RST, TON, TOF, CTU, RES. Addresses: I0.0 to I0.5, Q0.0 to Q0.3, M0.0 to M0.7, T1 to T8, C1 to C8. Timer presets are in seconds.
